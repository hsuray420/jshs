# Source registry

The machine-readable registry is [data/source-registry.json](data/source-registry.json). It is generated by `pnpm run generate:data-foundation` and is the single source-ID registry used by official-information, history validation and trust disclosures.

Sources are classified by dataset, district, school year, issuer, URL, ingestion mode, verification state and snapshot. The only hashed source snapshots currently available are the 15 locally stored 115 official guide PDFs. External portals without captured content have no hash and are not silently treated as announcements.

`history-community-*-115` sources remain community references. They cannot satisfy official-history validation.
